# HollyFrontier

> HollyFrontier Corporation was an independent petroleum refiner and marketer of gasoline,
> diesel fuel, jet fuel, asphalt and specialty lubricant products, headquartered in Dallas,
> Texas. In March 2022 it was reorganized under a new parent, HF Sinclair Corporation
> (NYSE: DINO), following the acquisition of Sinclair Oil. The HollyFrontier brand no longer
> operates independently: www.hollyfrontier.com returns HTTP 301 to www.hfsinclair.com.

## Status

- No public API, developer portal, SDK, webhook surface or machine-readable specification was
